Logical Model: BeEncounter Model

Official URL: https://www.ehealth.fgov.be/standards/fhir/core-clinical/StructureDefinition/BeModelEncounter Version: 1.2.0
Active as of 2026-08-18 Computable Name: BeModelEncounter

Administrative recording of a meeting between a patient and a practitioner/organisation, with references to clinical data.

Usages:

  • This Logical Model is not used by any profiles in this Specification

You can also check for usages in the FHIR IG Statistics

identifier 1..* Identifier Unique business identifier of the CareSet
recordedDate 1..1 dateTime Date of registration by the Author/Recorder (date of the last update).

status 1..1 code Registration status, the moment in the lifecycle. For example: final, corrected, cancelled, entered-in-error. ValueSet: VS_Encounter_Status
class 0..* CodeableConcept Classification of the type of Encounter. ValueSet: VS_Encounter_Class
serviceType 0..* CodeableConcept Type of services/specialisation offered during the Encounter. ValueSet: VS_Encounter_ServiceType (see proposal VS_Be_ServiceType_Voorstel)
subject 1..1 Reference() The person who is the subject of the healthcare the record (CareSet) refers to. Refers to BePatient
episodeOfCare 0..* Reference() Reference to the EpisodeOfCare resource this Encounter is linked to. Refers to BeEpisodeOfCare
basedOn 0..* Reference() The request that initiated this Encounter. In this case it can only originate from BeCarePlan
careTeam 0..* Reference() Reference to the CareTeam resource recording which team is involved in the care during this Encounter. Refers to BeCareTeam
partOf 0..1 Reference() Reference to another Encounter this Encounter is part of. Refers to BeEncounter
serviceProvider 0..1 Reference() Organisation responsible for this Encounter. Refers to BeOrganization
participant 0..* BackboneElement List of participants involved in the care during this Encounter. Refers to BePractitioner
period 1..1 Period Period during which the Participant is involved in this Encounter
role 1..1 CodeableConcept Role of the Participant involved in this Encounter
actualPeriod 0..1 Period The effective start and end moments of this Encounter
length 0..1 Duration Effective duration of the Encounter (excluding absences)
reason 0..* CodeableConcept The list of Reasons that will be discussed during this Encounter. ValueSet: VS_Encounter_Reason
diagnosis 0..* Reference() List of diagnoses relevant for this Encounter. Refers to BeProblem
admission 0..1 BackboneElement Details about the admission of the patient to the care organisation
origin 0..1 Reference() The location the patient came from for the admission. Refers to BeOrganization
admitSource 0..1 CodeableConcept How the patient was admitted. ValueSet: VS_Encounter_Admission_AdmitSource
reAdmission 0..1 boolean Is this a re-admission of the patient?
destination 0..1 Reference() Destination of the patient after discharge. Refers to BeOrganization
dischargeDisposition 0..1 CodeableConcept Category of location after discharge of the patient. ValueSet: VS_Encounter_Admission_DischargeDisposition
note 0..* Annotation Additional information related to the CareSet content in free text form
